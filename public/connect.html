Connections

Link your own accounts. Connecting Slack signs you in and lets Aspire Atlas read your own DMs and group DMs, so you can ask about them. Nobody else can see them in answers unless they're in the conversation too.

YOUR ACCOUNTS

Link your own accounts to sign in, include your private DMs, and get Jira results that follow your Jira permissions.

HOW IT WORKS
  1. Make sure you're signed in to Slack as yourself in this browser. The connection belongs to whoever Slack sees as signed in.
  2. Click Connect for each workspace you're in, then Allow in Slack.
  3. Sign out (on the main page) forgets you in this browser. Disconnect also revokes access and removes your DMs from the index, unless another participant is still connected.
